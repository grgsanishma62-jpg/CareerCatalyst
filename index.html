Every community has the power to make a difference. Through our proposed partnership with BYANJAN RESTAURANT & CAFE, MealShare Aldershot aims to explore how suitable surplus food can be put to better use.

Our goal is simple: reduce avoidable food waste and help good food reach people who may need support.

FROM SURPLUS TO SUPPORT

How It Works

Our proposed process is designed to make food sharing simple, safe and organised.

01

Food Is Identified

A participating food business identifies suitable surplus food that can be safely shared.

02

Collection Is Arranged

Collection details and food safety requirements are checked before any food is transferred.

03

Food Reaches People

The aim is to coordinate with appropriate support organisations so food can reach people who need it.

Food safety comes first. Any future food sharing must follow appropriate food safety, storage, allergen and handling guidance.
