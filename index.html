MEALSHARE ALDERSHOT

Good food should never go to waste.

We aim to connect suitable surplus food from local restaurants with people experiencing homelessness in our community. Because sharing a meal can make a difference.

WHY MEALSHARE?

Small actions can create big changes.

MealShare Aldershot is a community-focused project with two simple aims: reduce avoidable food waste and support people experiencing homelessness.

🌱

Reduce Food Waste

We want suitable surplus food to be used rather than unnecessarily thrown away at the end of the day.

🤝

Support Our Community

Our goal is to make suitable food available to people experiencing homelessness in Aldershot.

💚

Build Connections

We hope to work with more local restaurants and community organisations as the project develops.

OUR FIRST RESTAURANT PARTNER

Meet BYANJAN RESTAURANT & CAFE.

We are working with BYANJAN RESTAURANT & CAFE as our first restaurant partner. The project aims to collect suitable surplus food at the end of the day and redistribute it to people who need support.

This is the beginning of our journey. We hope this partnership will help us make a positive difference in the local community.

OUR APPROACH

How MealShare works.

Our planned process is simple. Every collection must be arranged safely with the restaurant.

01

Food is identified

The restaurant identifies suitable surplus food that may be available at the end of the day.

02

Food is collected

Collection is arranged and food is checked for safe handling, storage, allergens and suitability.

03

Food is shared

Suitable food is distributed to people experiencing homelessness through the project's planned arrangements.

Food safety comes first: Food will only be collected and shared when safe handling, storage, temperature, allergen information and collection arrangements have been agreed and checked.
