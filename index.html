OUR PURPOSE

Small actions. Shared kindness.

MealShare Aldershot is a student-led project exploring how surplus food from a local restaurant can be managed and shared with people experiencing food insecurity. The aim is to reduce avoidable food waste while helping useful food reach the community.

We are starting with one participating restaurant and will only list donations that have actually been offered.
