WHY WE'RE HERE

A little less waste. A little more kindness.

MealShare Aldershot is a community-focused project with a simple ambition: help good food find a useful purpose and support people experiencing homelessness.

OUR FIRST PARTNER

STARTING LOCALLY

Meet our first partner: BYANJAN RESTAURANT & CAFE.

We're beginning with BYANJAN RESTAURANT & CAFE. The aim is to explore how suitable surplus food could be collected at the end of the day and shared through safe, agreed arrangements.

This is the start of the journey. Any collection or distribution will only happen once the practical arrangements and food-safety requirements have been agreed.

THE PLAN

Simple steps. Thoughtful sharing.

Our planned process puts safety, dignity and clear communication first.

01 ◌

Identify

The restaurant identifies suitable surplus food that may be available to share.

02 ↗

Arrange collection

Collection is agreed and safe handling, storage and allergen information are checked.

03 ♡

Share responsibly

Suitable food is shared through the project's agreed distribution arrangements.

!

Safety comes first. Food will only be collected and shared when safe handling, storage, temperature, allergen information and collection arrangements have been checked.
